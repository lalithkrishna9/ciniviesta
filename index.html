What you'll take away

Five crafts.
One afternoon.

The session walks through the stages of making a film, with a working director answering from experience.

Craft

Storytelling

Finding the story, shaping it, and holding an audience from first scene to last.

Craft

Screenplay

How an idea becomes pages that a crew and cast can actually shoot.

Craft

Direction

Leading a set, working with actors and making decisions under pressure.

Craft

Acting

What performance looks like from both sides of the camera, with actors on stage.

Craft

Visual language

Framing, movement and mood: how a film says things without words.

  • Practical knowledge of filmmaking and storytelling.
  • A clear picture of the stages of creating a film.
  • Lessons from real experience in the film industry.
  • A push to explore filmmaking as a creative career.
